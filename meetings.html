First Time?

What to expect at a meeting

👋
Everyone Is Welcome

You don't need to be a member to attend — only to vote. Come listen, ask questions, and see what the PTA is working on. Kids welcome.

⏰
About an Hour

Meetings run roughly 60 minutes: a short agenda, treasurer's report, program updates, and votes on anything that needs member approval.

🗳️
Your Vote Counts

Members vote on the budget, officers, and major spending. It's the most direct way to shape what the PTA does for your child's school.
