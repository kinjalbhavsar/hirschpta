FAQ

Membership questions

Do I have to volunteer or attend meetings if I join?

No. Membership carries zero obligations — many members simply pay dues and enjoy knowing they've funded programs for every student. Meetings and volunteering are always open to you, never required.

How much does it cost?

Current dues are listed on our TOTEM membership page. Cost should never keep a family from joining — email pta.hirsch@gmail.com and we'll work something out, no questions asked.

Who can join?

Everyone who cares about Hirsch students: parents, guardians, grandparents, aunts and uncles, teachers, staff, and community members. You do not need a child enrolled at Hirsch.

Can both parents join? Should we?

Yes, and it helps! Each membership adds a vote and additional support for programs. Grandparents and other family members are welcome too.

Is membership the same as donating?

They're separate. Dues make you a member with voting rights; a portion supports the state and national PTA. Donations are 100% local and tax-deductible — you can do either or both. See ways to give.

When does membership expire?

Memberships run for one school year and renew each fall. Join any time — dues are the same year-round.
